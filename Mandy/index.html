Meet Mandy!

Scientific Name:

Mandy

Discovered by / at:

Haya Faisal, August 2024

Family:

Mandy does not have any immediate family, however somewhere in other HydroOrbs there might be some creatures that can be classified as his family.

Habitat:

HydroOrbs in Multiraidle.

Appearance:

Mandy's body is made out of atoms, with its head being a very large hollow shape and its tail being a very small hollow shape - almost invisible. Since it is always moving in its habitat, it looks like a circle with diameters ranging between 2-10 appearing and disappearing.

Key Characteristics:

  • It is happy when in a HydroOrbs and sad when out of it.
  • It swims fast when happy.
  • It swims very slow when sad.
  • ...

What we know so far about Mandy? Mandy is a very mysterious creature and has just been found. It is also a very introverted, shy creature, with a very small lifespan when it is without a HydroOrbs. All we know about Mandy is that it lives in a HydroOrbs.It's HydroOrbs can dry up due to rising temperatures. If it is without a HydroOrbs it needs to be fed food or needs to be bought to a new HydroOrbs. If it is not fed food or is without a HydroOrbs for 4 mins, it will die.
Guidelines for human interaction with this creature: When Mandy loses its HydroOrbs, please feed him food or drag the water droplet to it. If Mandy is not fed food for 2 minutes or is without a HydroOrbs for 4 mins, it will die.
