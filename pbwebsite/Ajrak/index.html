Sindhi Ajrak is a traditional block-printed shawl that holds deep cultural and historical significance for the Sindhi people, primarily from the Sindh region in Pakistan. It is characterized by its unique patterns, typically in deep red, black, white, and indigo colors. The motifs often include geometric and floral designs, created using a complex dyeing process that includes resist printing and natural dyes.

Ajrak is more than just a textile—it's a symbol of Sindhi heritage, used in various cultural celebrations, given as a gesture of respect or hospitality, and worn by both men and women in different forms, like shawls, turbans, or stoles. It is also closely associated with Sindhi identity and pride, celebrated worldwide wherever Sindhi communities reside.
