Dabki

The dabki is a traditional bread plate used in Sindh, Pakistan, to serve flatbreads such as roti or naan. This simple yet culturally significant item is an essential part of Sindhi dining traditions, symbolizing hospitality and community. Typically circular and shallow, the dabki is crafted from materials like brass, stainless steel, or clay, depending on its intended use and the resources available.

The design of the dabki is often modest, focusing on functionality, though some versions feature subtle engravings or patterns that reflect the region’s artistic heritage. These plates are durable and lightweight, making them convenient for everyday use in households and traditional gatherings. During meals, a dabki is used to present bread separately, ensuring its texture remains intact while complementing the other dishes on the table, such as curry, lentils, or pickles.
